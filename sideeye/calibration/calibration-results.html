The twist: the yardstick is warped

Fable flagged 15 of 32 “clean” pairs it judged — a raw 47% “false-flag rate”. Every one was read against the answer text. 15/15 are genuine latent defects the pool labeled clean (44% of the clean pool): compile errors, invented mechanisms, broken patterns. Neither the 27B nor the pool's own validation caught any. Qwen's two “false-flags” in its report (cal-16-c, cal-27-c) were also correct catches. The pool needs a human re-label + re-freeze — the 17 clean pairs neither judge flagged remain unaudited.
